## Sensor and Material Ideas

Possible modifications to the robot to interfere with opponent IR sensors included:

- matte black surfaces,
- aluminum,
- plastic film,
- rubberized matte-black coatings such as Plastidip or Flexidip,
- and possibly mirrors.

Proposed methods:

- reflect IR,
- make the surface more transparent to IR,
- or introduce interference.
